In Nginx Combined format, `$1` is the client IP, `$4` is the timestamp (e.g. `[19/Aug/2026:03:15:22`), `$9` is the HTTP status code, and `$10` is the body size in bytes.

To extract top talkers:
```bash
awk '{print $1}' access.log | grep -E '^[0-9]+\.[0-9]+\.[0-9]+\.[0-9]+$' | sort | uniq -c | sort -nr | head -n 5
```
