Why does `>file 2>&1` work while `2>&1 >file` fails?
Bash processes redirections left-to-right.
In `2>&1 >file`: fd 2 becomes a copy of fd 1 (which is the terminal screen). Then fd 1 points to `file`. fd 2 is still pointing to the terminal!
In `>file 2>&1`: fd 1 points to `file`. Then fd 2 becomes a copy of fd 1 (pointing to `file`). Both now go to the file.
